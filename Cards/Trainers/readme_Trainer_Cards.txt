These Trainer (regular and leader) cards are shuffled in with their given color decks.
The fronts and corresponding backs are given in the same order.

The PDF files here are for self-printing onto tabloid-sized paper and cutting out at home. 
--> They are in RGB format, not CMYK. That's because the self-service printers at Kinko's / FedEx Office do their own color correction in firmware. Giving those printers CMYK will cause the colors to print too dark.
Put the card cutouts and their corresponding backs in clear card sleeves with normal-sized poker cards sandwiched between.

For professional print-on-demand services, use the PNG files in the accompanying folder. They are 300 DPI. 
--> Put them into the template provided by your printing vendor.
--> 1/8th inch on white border bleed has been added to them.
