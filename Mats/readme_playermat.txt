

This player mat can be printed and cut out. It is meant to be 7.75 inches tall and 16.25 inches wide.
There is a 1/8th inch safety zone inside the edges of the mat.
There is also a 1/8th inch color bleed outside the edges.

The PDF file here is for self-printing onto tabloid-sized paper and cutting out at home.
--> It is in RGB format, not CMYK. That's because the self-service printers at Kinko's / FedEx Office do their own color correction in firmware. Giving those printers CMYK will cause the colors to print too dark.

For professional print-on-demand services, use the PNG file. It is 300 DPI. 
--> Put the PNG into the template provided by your printing vendor.
--> There is one without cut marks and one with cut marks, according to your preference.
--> There is also one with cut marks and two mats together to get more efficiency out of paper use.

The Lefthand and Righthand PNG files are for the Launch Tabletop print-on-demand service.
For using Launch, have them be made as a custom-sized 4-page booklet (basically one sheet of paper that is folded over).

The player_mat_printable_double_18x18 is for the boardgamesmaker.com 18 by 18 inch paper mat.
