
Black dice are very common. You should have no problem finding black dice with white pips.
Otherwise, you can always substitute the classic white dice with black pips.

This readme is more for the red dice and blue dice. They are not very common.

For making blue dice, the easiest thing is to buy a blue "d3". 
That is to say, a blue die numbered 1 through 3 used for tabletop roleplaying games.

The red dice will be difficult to buy without custom-ordering it. 
It is effectively a "d3 + 3" being numbered 4 through 6.

Therefore, PNG files have been provided to make stickers. 
Print them on sticker paper, cut them out, and put them on regular dice or on blank cubes made of wood or plastic.
The PNG files are 600 dpi. They are intended to make 13mm by 13mm square stickers with 3mm of color bleed for each edge.
They are meant to be stuck onto dice that are 16mm, the most common size.

The launch_tabletop folder has PNG files that are 300 DPI ready for the Launch Tabletop pint-on-demand service.
